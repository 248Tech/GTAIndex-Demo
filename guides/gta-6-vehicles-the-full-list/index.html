GTA 6 vehicles: cars, bikes and boats so far

The vehicles shown in the GTA 6 reveal and Extended Look, plus every car in the database — cars, bikes, boats and off-roaders across Leonida.

Rockstar hasn't published a full Grand Theft Auto VI vehicle list, but the Extended Look put plenty on screen, and the series has a huge, class-based garage. Here's where things stand — browse the vehicles database for each entry.

Seen in the reveal

The trailers and Extended Look show a wide spread of ways to get around Leonida:

  • Sports cars and muscle tearing through Vice City.
  • Dirt bikes and motorcycles, including stunt riding.
  • Boats and jet skis across the bays and keys.
  • Off-roaders in the wetlands.

From superbikes to supercars, Leonida's garage runs deep.

In the database so far

We're tracking confirmed and edition vehicles as they're revealed:

  • Grotti Cheetah — a Grotti supercar.
  • Vapid Buggy — an off-roader.
  • Vapid Ganado Retro — a retro pickup.
  • Vapid Stanier — a classic sedan from the Vintage Vice City pack.

Class-based, like always

Grand Theft Auto vehicles fall into familiar classes — super, sports, muscle, off-road, motorcycles, boats, aircraft and more — each with its own handling. We add stats and details to each entry as Rockstar confirms them.

The full line-up is unconfirmed until launch. Check the vehicles database for the latest.

Verified vehicle-system additions

Vehicles are more class-specific: drivetrain, tyres, suspension and steering feed the physics model, muscle cars have a more distinctive feel, supercars are poor off-road choices, and higher top speeds are reported. Fuel and EV charging are confirmed, while roads are denser and narrower, making driving more deliberate. Vehicle developers include members of Rockstar’s racing team.

Different workshops specialise in different modification types, while Pay ’n’ Spray is limited to repairs and basic resprays intended for evading police. A Vehicle tab gives quicker access to car controls, and the reported Scooter Bros service delivers a personal vehicle through an in-world NPC.

Source: Verified preview update, 1 Sep 2026 — Dazed interview and Rockstar North creator-preview material supplied to GTAIndex.
