GTA 6 FAQ: every big question answered before launch

Release date, unlock time, pre-load, download size, price, editions, PS5 Pro, 60fps, PC, online and more — every common GTA 6 question answered with what Rockstar has actually confirmed.

Five weeks to go. These are the questions we get asked most about Grand Theft Auto VI, answered with what Rockstar, PlayStation and Xbox have actually confirmed — and clearly marked where something is still unknown. Each answer links to a deeper guide.

Release and launch

When does GTA 6 come out?

November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. More in our release date and platforms guide.

What time does GTA 6 unlock?

Rockstar hasn't published a global unlock time. Pre-orders and pre-loading both start at local midnight, and store listings point to the game unlocking at midnight in each region — but treat that as likely, not official, until your store shows it. See the launch-day guide.

Can I pre-load GTA 6?

Yes. Digital pre-orders can pre-load from local midnight on November 12, 2026, a week before launch. Boxed copies reach retailers from November 12 so their codes can pre-load too.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Not announced yet. The exact size will show in your console's download queue once pre-loading starts. Clear plenty of space in advance — our launch-day guide explains how.

Does the boxed version have a disc?

No. Rockstar confirms the physical version contains a download code inside the box — no disc. You'll need an internet connection to download the game.

Has GTA 6 been delayed?

It has moved before: an original 2025 target, then May 2026, then November 19, 2026. That's the date Rockstar is currently standing by.

Price, editions and bonuses

How much does GTA 6 cost?

In the US, the Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99. Prices are set per region, so check your store. Full breakdown: GTA 6 editions and price compared.

What's the difference between Standard and Ultimate?

Standard is the game. Ultimate adds the Ultimate Edition Upgrade — premium vehicles like the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers, extra shops and businesses, and more, threaded through the story. Every item is listed in our Ultimate Edition guide.

Can I upgrade to the Ultimate Edition later?

Yes. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade can be bought separately at any time from the PlayStation or Microsoft store. Boxed-copy owners can upgrade once their download code has been redeemed. There's no physical Ultimate Edition.

What are the pre-order bonuses?

All pre-orders and purchases before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack — the '55 Vapid Stanier and Shore Court garage, outfits and hairstyles, and a weapon pattern. Digital pre-orders also get a free month of GTA+. Pre-order bonus details.

Are GTA 6 codes region-locked?

On PlayStation, yes — a code must match the region of your PlayStation account. On Xbox, Rockstar says codes are not region-locked. Buy from a store in your account's country to be safe.

Platforms and performance

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not announced. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both reached PC later, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed a PC version of GTA VI. PC release guide.

Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. It's PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only.

Is GTA 6 enhanced for PS5 Pro?

PlayStation lists the game as PS5 Pro Enhanced, but neither Rockstar nor Sony has said exactly what that adds. Performance guide.

Will GTA 6 run at 60fps?

No 60fps mode has been announced. The Extended Look and creator previews ran at 30fps, and Rockstar has described current console builds as 30fps. Performance guide.

Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

Rockstar describes Grand Theft Auto VI as a single-player experience. No online mode has been announced for launch. (GTA+ — included for a month with digital pre-orders — currently applies to GTA Online for GTA V and Rockstar's games library.)

The game

Who are the main characters?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a criminal couple you can switch between. Meet them and the rest of the cast in the characters guide.

Where is GTA 6 set?

The state of Leonida, a fictional Florida, centred on a modern-day Vice City, with the Keys, the Grassrivers wetlands, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga around it. Explore it on our interactive map or read Leonida explained.

Is there a GTA 6 soundtrack?

Yes — six radio stations have been revealed, the game adds podcasts and on-foot listening, and Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (34 tracks) launches November 19. Radio and soundtrack.

Is there official GTA 6 merch?

Yes. The Grand Theft Auto VI Collection (tees, caps, keychain, money clip, lighter, totes, stickers) launched on October 8, and the Goodtime State – Vice City Collection collector's box arrives November 19. Merch guide.

Using GTAIndex at launch

Will the GTAIndex map be updated when the game is out?

Yes. Today the map is built on the community's reconstruction of Leonida from official footage. From launch we'll mark places as verified in-game, add collectible locations and update the map as the real one is charted. How to use the map.

How can I track my 100% completion?

Use the collectibles checklists and My Progress. They already list every expected collectible set and will fill in with real locations from launch day.
