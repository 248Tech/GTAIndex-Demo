GTA 6 PC Release: Is Grand Theft Auto VI Coming to PC?

GTA 6 PC has not been announced. Here is what Rockstar confirms for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, plus what GTA 5 and RDR2 history suggests.

Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 PC version. As of October 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI is officially confirmed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a launch date of November 19, 2026.

There is currently no confirmed PC release date, PC launch window, PC pre-order or official announcement that a Windows version is in development. Rockstar's history makes a later PC release plausible, but that remains an expectation rather than a confirmed plan.

GTA 6 Is Officially Confirmed for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

Rockstar currently lists only two console families for GTA VI:

  • PlayStation 5
  • Xbox Series X|S

The game is scheduled to launch on both platforms on November 19, 2026.

Rockstar's official support documentation, updated on August 18, 2026, states that GTA VI can be pre-ordered for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Its official store likewise lists the November 19 release date without offering a PC platform option.

For a broader breakdown of the launch, see our GTA 6 release date and platforms guide.

Vice City nightlife — PS5 and Xbox Series only, for now.

There Is No GTA 6 PC Release Date

At present, Rockstar has announced no GTA 6 PC release date.

That also means there is currently:

  • No official GTA 6 PC release window.
  • No GTA 6 PC pre-order.
  • No confirmed PC system requirements.
  • No announced Steam or Epic Games Store version.
  • No official Rockstar Games Launcher pre-order for GTA VI on PC.

Any website, retailer listing, social-media post or video claiming to know an exact GTA 6 PC launch date is therefore going beyond what Rockstar has publicly confirmed.

Until Rockstar makes an announcement, specific PC dates should be treated as rumor or speculation.

The GTA 6 Extended Look Was Captured on PlayStation 5

Rockstar's August 27, 2026 Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look presentation does not provide evidence of a PC build.

Rockstar explicitly states that the entire presentation was captured from in-game footage on PlayStation 5.

That is worth emphasizing because high-quality GTA VI footage can easily lead to speculation that Rockstar is demonstrating the game on a powerful PC.

In this case, Rockstar has directly identified the platform used for the Extended Look as PS5.

It does not prove that a PC version does or does not exist internally. It simply means the public presentation was captured on PlayStation 5 and offers no official PC announcement.

Will GTA 6 Eventually Come to PC?

A GTA 6 PC version is certainly plausible based on Rockstar's previous major releases, but Rockstar has not promised one.

The strongest argument for an eventual PC port comes from the company's history.

Two of Rockstar's biggest recent releases arrived on consoles first before eventually coming to PC:

  • Grand Theft Auto V launched on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in September 2013. Its PC version arrived on April 14, 2015.
  • Red Dead Redemption 2 launched on PlayStation 4 and Xbox One in October 2018. Its PC version followed on November 5, 2019.

Both cases demonstrate Rockstar's willingness to release major games on PC after an initial console launch.

They do not establish a guaranteed timetable for GTA VI.

How Long Did GTA 5 Take to Come to PC?

Grand Theft Auto V originally launched for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 on September 17, 2013.

Rockstar released the PC version on April 14, 2015.

That is approximately 19 months after the original console release, often rounded to around a year and a half.

The gap was also complicated by the arrival of the PlayStation 4 and Xbox One editions in November 2014, which preceded the PC release by several months.

For GTA VI, there is no evidence that Rockstar intends to repeat the same schedule.

How Long Did Red Dead Redemption 2 Take to Come to PC?

Red Dead Redemption 2 launched on PlayStation 4 and Xbox One on October 26, 2018.

Rockstar subsequently announced a PC edition, which arrived on November 5, 2019.

The gap was a little over 12 months.

Its PC version included technical and graphical improvements, additional settings and platform-specific enhancements, demonstrating that Rockstar has previously used delayed PC launches to offer more extensive configuration options.

Again, that history is useful context rather than a roadmap for GTA VI.

Could GTA 6 PC Arrive a Year After Consoles?

It is possible, but there is currently no reliable basis for assigning a specific date.

Using Rockstar's two most relevant examples:

Game Console Launch PC Launch Approximate Gap
GTA V September 17, 2013 April 14, 2015 19 months
Red Dead Redemption 2 October 26, 2018 November 5, 2019 12 months
GTA VI November 19, 2026 Not announced Unknown

If GTA VI followed a similar pattern, a PC version could theoretically arrive sometime after the console release.

However, using those previous gaps to predict a specific 2027 or 2028 GTA 6 PC release date would still be speculation.

Development requirements, commercial strategy and Rockstar's launch plans can differ substantially between games.

Why Does Rockstar Release PC Versions Later?

Rockstar has not published a GTA VI-specific explanation for the lack of a PC announcement, so claims about the reason should be treated cautiously.

Historically, delayed PC releases can give developers additional time to account for the much wider variety of PC hardware configurations and to develop platform-specific technical features.

For example, Rockstar's GTA V PC release included:

  • Extensive graphics settings.
  • Higher-resolution support.
  • 60fps support.
  • Mouse and keyboard customization.
  • Multi-monitor support.
  • Adjustable population-density settings.

The PC edition of Red Dead Redemption 2 likewise arrived with graphical and technical enhancements.

That history may help explain why fans expect GTA VI to receive a dedicated PC edition eventually, but Rockstar has not said that this is its current strategy for GTA VI.

Can You Pre-Order GTA 6 on PC?

No official GTA VI PC pre-order currently exists.

Rockstar's current pre-order options are for:

  • PlayStation 5.
  • Xbox Series X|S.

If a website offers what it describes as a guaranteed GTA 6 PC pre-order, buyers should verify whether it is actually authorized by Rockstar before paying.

The safest indication that PC pre-orders are legitimate will be a PC listing directly from Rockstar or an officially announced digital storefront.

What Platforms Will GTA 6 Be On at Launch?

Based on Rockstar's current announcement, GTA VI launches on:

  • PS5
  • Xbox Series X|S

There is no announced launch-day PC edition.

There is also no announced version for PlayStation 4 or Xbox One.

Rockstar's official GTA VI platform support page specifically directs customers to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S pre-orders.

Should PC Players Wait for GTA 6?

That depends on whether playing at launch matters more than waiting for a potential PC edition.

Anyone who wants to play GTA VI on its confirmed November 19, 2026 release date will currently need access to a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S.

PC-only players have two practical options:

  • Wait for Rockstar to announce a PC edition.
  • Play the console version at launch and consider a later PC edition if one is eventually released.

Buying a console solely because you expect a delayed PC release to take a particular number of months would be risky because Rockstar has not provided a PC timetable.

Is GTA 6 Coming to Steam?

Rockstar has not announced a GTA VI Steam release.

There is currently no confirmed GTA VI PC version at all, so its potential PC storefronts are also unknown.

Previous Rockstar PC games such as GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 are available through PC storefronts including Steam, but that precedent does not confirm how GTA VI would be distributed.

Any Steam release date currently circulating should therefore be considered unconfirmed.

Is GTA 6 Coming to the Rockstar Games Launcher?

There is no confirmed GTA VI listing for PC through the Rockstar Games Launcher.

Rockstar has used its own launcher for previous PC releases, including Red Dead Redemption 2, so it would be an obvious potential distribution platform if GTA VI eventually reaches PC.

However, Rockstar has made no such announcement.

GTA 6 PC Release FAQ

Has Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 for PC?

No. Rockstar currently confirms GTA VI for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

There is no official GTA 6 PC release date or release window.

Will GTA 6 release on PC on November 19, 2026?

Not according to Rockstar's current platform announcements. November 19, 2026 is the confirmed launch date for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Was the GTA 6 Extended Look running on PC?

No PC capture was announced. Rockstar says the Extended Look was captured entirely from in-game footage on PlayStation 5.

Did GTA 5 launch on PC at the same time as consoles?

No. GTA V first launched on consoles in September 2013 and reached PC on April 14, 2015, approximately 19 months later.

Did Red Dead Redemption 2 launch on PC at the same time as consoles?

No. Red Dead Redemption 2 launched on consoles in October 2018 and arrived on PC on November 5, 2019, a little over one year later.

Does Rockstar's history guarantee GTA 6 will come to PC?

No. Previous PC releases make a later GTA VI PC version plausible, but Rockstar has not officially announced one.

Conclusion

The current answer to the GTA 6 PC question is straightforward: Rockstar has not announced a PC version of Grand Theft Auto VI.

GTA VI is officially scheduled to launch on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar's Extended Look was captured entirely from in-game footage on PS5. There is no PC release date, launch window or official PC pre-order.

GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both reached PC after their original console releases, so history provides a reasonable basis for expecting that GTA VI could eventually do the same. It does not provide a reliable date.

For anyone determined to play on launch day, PS5 or Xbox Series X|S are currently the only confirmed options. This page should be updated as soon as Rockstar announces anything official about a GTA VI PC version.
