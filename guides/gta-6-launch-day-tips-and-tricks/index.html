GTA 6 launch-day tips and tricks

Launch-day tips for GTA 6 sorted by how well they are sourced: stated in beginner guides, reported by players, or not yet known. Covers Focus Mode, cash, robberies, travel, saves and more.

Launch-day tips for Grand Theft Auto VI, collected from beginner guides and launch-day coverage. Each tip says where it comes from. Rockstar hasn't confirmed any of these, so treat them as player and press findings.

Stated in guides

These come from written beginner guides. They describe the game as the guide writers saw it, not from a Rockstar statement.

  • Buy a suppressor early. Ammu-Nation sells suppressors for most guns, and a store in Key Lento, after the tutorial, is cheap enough to afford early.
  • Get a garage after Key Lento. Stored vehicles are free to keep, and the garage gives you access to your loadout and a place to drop off illegal goods.
  • Stash cash in Jason's bedroom. Until your first bank account (from the Buck Me mission), loose floorboards in his bedroom hold your money.
  • Use Focus Mode. Click both sticks. With a gun it slows time briefly. Enemies show red, items yellow and cash green. White doors can be entered, red doors are alarmed.
  • Health, Stamina and Focus. Lifting raises Health, cardio raises Stamina, and fishing or pool raise Focus. Health and Stamina gains fade, but Focus is permanent.
  • Buffs. Eating, sleeping and showering give temporary buffs. Feeding Jason's pet snake gives a small Focus buff.
  • Sleep to skip the night. Sleep at Jason's house.
  • Travel. There's no map warp. RydeMe skips the ride for an extra $100, and switching characters is the cheapest travel.
  • Car theft. Hold up on the D-pad to open the Waink app and check a car's security. Trackers and alarms apply, and keyless cars need a key cloner.
  • Robberies. Scout first, use the back door with a snap gun to avoid alarms, and skip banks and loan stores until Chapter 3 (dye packs).
  • Death costs you. Dying loses your cash and stash bags. You can pass bags to a partner with the D-pad.
  • Fences. Yasmani in Little Cuba pays well for jewellery and marked bills. Tyrone pays for narcotics.
  • Criminal profile. Heat comes from appearance, outfit, weapon, vehicle, CCTV and partner. CCTV is destroyed in a back room. The profile has five levels, and Irredeemable is permanent.
  • Non-lethal play. Head and heart hits kill, and limb hits injure. Shooting out tyres makes pursuers spin out.
  • Character switching. The character you're not playing usually waits near your safehouse.
  • Garage and cars. Refuel and repair cars to raise their resale value. A bank account is needed to sell cars and keep cash safe. Slim Jim breaks into cars, and clone keys avoid repeated hotwiring.
  • Locations. A military base near the starting zone has a tank, and the VCIA airport tarmac has a commercial jet to take.
  • Saves. There are 15 save slots. Autosaves happen after missions and some open-world encounters, including Wasted or arrest. Saving during missions doesn't seem to be possible.

Player reports

These come from social posts, Reddit threads or named people, relayed by press. They haven't been reproduced or confirmed.

  • A next mission can be found in the Map screen's mission list.
  • The RydeMe app gives instant travel.
  • Hold down on the D-pad for quick access to nearby locations and objectives.
  • The Scooter Bros phone app can deliver a registered car to you.
  • Register a stolen car you want to keep at a Pay 'N' Spray.
  • Stealing a car with a cat in it can make the cat attack.
  • Stashing guns may de-escalate police encounters.
  • On base PS5, one Reddit user says clearing the cache and turning off HDCP fixes stutter. The same report warns that turning off HDCP breaks streaming and Blu-ray.
  • Framerate may dip below 30fps on base PS5 during action. Not confirmed.
  • Some players find the default stick dead zone too large, which can make the game feel sluggish. You can change it in controller settings.

Not yet known

  • Whether GTA 6 Online launches on day one, and what it includes.
  • Whether there's a PC version, and when. Editors have only guessed.
  • The unlock time and download size are still unannounced by Rockstar. See our launch guide.
