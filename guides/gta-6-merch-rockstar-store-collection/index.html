Official GTA 6 merch: the Rockstar Store collection and collector’s box

Rockstar’s Grand Theft Auto VI Collection is out: VI and Macca the Gator tees, caps, a Vice City Zippo and money clip, keychain, totes and stickers — plus the Goodtime State collector’s box arriving November 19.

Rockstar has opened the merch floodgates ahead of launch. On October 8, 2026 it released the official Grand Theft Auto VI Collection at the Rockstar Store, two weeks after opening pre-orders for a limited-edition collector's box. Here's everything in both.

The Grand Theft Auto VI Collection (October 8)

Available exclusively from the Rockstar Store:

  • VI and Rockstar Games logo tees — heavyweight cotton in black, blue and pink, with either the VI logo or the classic Rockstar logo.
  • Goodtime State and Macca the Gator tees — "Goodtime State Gear" inspired by the styles Jason and Lucia wear in the Ultimate Edition, featuring characters from Leonida's hit TV show Macca the Gator.
  • Flexfit caps — stretch-fit cotton caps with palm trees inside the VI logo, plus the original Rockstar cap in GTA VI colours. The caps are pre-order.
  • Keychain — chrome, with the Rockstar logo laser-etched on one side and the palm-tree VI logo on the other.
  • Vice City money clip — two-sided chrome with the classic Vice City logo and the VI logo.
  • Vice City Zippo lighter — refillable, polished chrome.
  • Canvas tote bags and two sets of vinyl stickers.

Rockstar says more items are coming to the collection. The store also has a new wishlist feature, and Rockstar mailing-list subscribers can get restock and sale alerts.

The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection (collector's box)

Announced on September 24, 2026, this is a limited-edition collector's box that arrives on November 19 and is available to pre-order while supplies last. Inspired by Macca the Gator, it includes:

  • a 6-inch Macca the Gator figure with a stash compartment
  • matte black GTAVI Oakley Frogskins sunglasses with Prizm Sapphire lenses
  • a New Era 9FORTY A-Frame snapback with Vice City Leonida details
  • a Macca the Gator magnetic mirror
  • a Vice City swizzle spoon
  • a Vice City razor blade keychain
  • a Leonida Keys crossbody bag
  • a Chunkee the Manatee shot glass
  • a Leonida Keys enamel pin set in a custom metal tin
  • premium stickers in a stash bag
  • a double-sided souvenir poster of the full Leonida landscape

The game is not included — this is merch, not a collector's edition of GTA VI. It's widely reported at $399.99 in the US; check the store for your region's price. Rockstar said pre-order details for Asia, Latin America and Australia would follow.

Where to buy

Only at store.rockstargames.com. Availability and prices differ by country, and some items may not ship to every region.

Also out on November 19

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album — 34 original tracks with Atlantic Records, on vinyl and CD as well as streaming. Radio stations and soundtrack.
