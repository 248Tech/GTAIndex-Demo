Zombix

Health-restoring drink shown in the Extended Look.

Zombix is a health-restoring consumable shown in GTA VI’s Extended Look. An on-screen warning notes it becomes *less* effective the more you use it in a short span, so you can’t spam it to top up health indefinitely. It sits in the quick-use item slots alongside an EpiPen-style injector. The exact amount it restores is not confirmed — the value shown here is an estimate.

Effects

Health +0
Stamina +0
Focus +0

Health is calibrated to GTA V / GTA Online equivalents (snacks restore health; a medkit ≈ a full heal). Stamina and Focus are new GTA VI HUD stats with no GTA V consumable analog, so those are estimates. Updated when Rockstar confirms official figures.
